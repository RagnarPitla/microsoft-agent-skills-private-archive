# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this repository to be a place where anyone can propose, review or question a skill
without being made to feel unwelcome for it. As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps this project:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, especially in a review
  thread where a skill's judgement is being challenged.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for readers of the skills, not for any one contributor's preference.
- Naming a disagreement plainly rather than letting it fester across several PRs.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information - including anything covered by
  [.agents/confidentiality.md](./.agents/confidentiality.md) - without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, issue or pull request.
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions -
and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainer, Ragnar Pitla, via the
contact methods listed on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/RagnarPitla) or by opening a
confidential [security advisory](https://github.com/RagnarPitla/microsoft-agent-skills/security/advisories/new)
if the report itself needs to stay private. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
